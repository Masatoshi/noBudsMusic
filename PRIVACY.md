# Privacy Policy for noBudsMusic

Last updated: 2026-08-12

noBudsMusic does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The app runs locally on macOS. It does not include analytics, advertising,
user accounts, a network service, or a remote data store. Preferences such as
whether the protection is enabled, whether the menu bar item is shown, and
whether the app launches at login are stored locally by macOS for the app.

noBudsMusic receives media-control events delivered by macOS so it can act as
the current Now Playing destination. Those events are handled locally and are
not recorded or sent anywhere.

This policy applies to the noBudsMusic macOS application distributed by Kaizu
Electric Co., Ltd. If the app's data practices change, this policy will be
updated before the changed version is distributed.

For questions about this policy, open an issue in the project's support
repository: <https://github.com/Masatoshi/noBudsMusic>.
